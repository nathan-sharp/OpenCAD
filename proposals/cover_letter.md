# Cover Letter: Sponsorship Application for OCIS

**Date:** [Insert Date]
**To:** Standardization Sponsorship Committee
**From:** OpenCAD Maintainers

**Subject:** Request for Sponsorship to Advance OpenCAD Interchange Standard (OCIS) to Formal Standardization

Dear Committee Members,

We are writing to formally request sponsorship for the OpenCAD Interchange Standard (OCIS) as it transitions from a public working draft to an official standard recognized by international bodies.

OCIS tackles the significant industry challenge of proprietary fragmentation across mechanical CAD, electrical design, and simulation fields. Over the past development cycles, we have successfully released the normative draft, completed the JSON schemas, provided multi-implementation proof of interoperability with Python and C++ reference parsers, and established a validation testing framework.

Sponsorship from your organization will provide the administrative guidance and critical resources necessary to refine the specification through a formal consensus process and bring it before bodies such as ISO/TC 184/SC 4.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
The OpenCAD Interchange Standard Maintainers
